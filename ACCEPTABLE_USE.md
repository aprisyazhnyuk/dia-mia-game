# Acceptable Use Policy

“Dia Mia vs The World” is a personal creative project shared publicly for entertainment, learning and respectful noncommercial experimentation.

Please use the project and its materials in a way that respects the people, characters and personal experiences represented in it.

## Welcome uses

Examples of welcome uses include:

- playing and sharing the original game;
- studying how the game works;
- creating respectful noncommercial modifications;
- using the project for personal learning or experimentation;
- discussing or reviewing the game with appropriate attribution.

## Unwelcome uses

Please do not use the project, its characters, artwork or creative content for:

- harassment, bullying, threats or targeted abuse;
- hateful, degrading, defamatory or deliberately humiliating material;
- sexualized or exploitative depictions of real or represented people;
- impersonation, deepfakes or content intended to mislead others about its origin;
- exposing private information, stalking, surveillance or biometric identification;
- training facial-recognition, identity-replication or impersonation systems;
- scams, malware, phishing or other deceptive activity;
- promotion of violence, discrimination or illegal activity;
- presenting modified content as official or endorsed by the project’s creator or subjects.

## Commercial use

Commercial use is prohibited under the repository’s licenses unless separate written permission has been obtained from the copyright holder.

## Attribution and modifications

When sharing permitted adaptations:

- provide the attribution required by `ASSET_LICENSE.md`;
- make it clear that the work has been modified;
- do not imply endorsement by Anton Prisiazhniuk, Dia Mia, or anyone represented by the project;
- link to the original repository when reasonably possible.

## Relationship to the licenses

This document describes the project’s community expectations. It does not replace or modify the legal rights and obligations established by `LICENSE` or `ASSET_LICENSE.md`.

## Concerns

If you encounter misuse of the project or need permission for a use not clearly covered here, contact the repository owner through GitHub.
